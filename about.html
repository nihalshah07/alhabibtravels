Our Story & Vision

More Than a Travel Agency—Your Trusted Companion Since 1997

Al-Habib Travels is one of the oldest and most respected travel agencies in Jammu & Kashmir. Originally founded as YIR Holidays in 1997, we have operated under the brand name Al-Habib Travels since 2011 with full-service offices in Srinagar and New Delhi.

Determined to stand as a symbol of trust, excellence, and reliability, our experienced specialists treat every traveler like extended family—while actively investing in upcoming hospitality and luxury hotel projects across Kashmir (currently 50% complete) to create meaningful career opportunities for local youth.
